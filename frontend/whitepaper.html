01. Market Problem & Thesis

Decentralized memecoin launches suffer from four primary vulnerabilities:

  • Liquidity Drain (Rug Pull): Developers seed liquidity on an AMM, collect retail capital, and then remove the underlying LP tokens.
  • Insider Pre-Allocations: Private minting of 20% to 50% of the total supply to team wallets before public launch.
  • Reentrancy & Honeypots: Hidden minting capabilities, address blacklists, or malicious tax overrides in unaudited ERC-20 bytecode.
  • Inefficient Rebase Reflections: Legacy reflection tokens (e.g. SafeMoon) break compatibility with Uniswap V3/V4 pools and trigger massive gas costs.
Why Robinhood Chain? Robinhood Chain operates on the Arbitrum Nitro rollup stack, delivering sub-second block times and sub-cent transaction fees. This allows retail participants to trade, claim dividends, and graduate pools without prohibitive Ethereum L1 gas costs.

03. Mathematical Model & Bonding Curve Mechanics

Robin Pump uses a Virtual Constant-Product AMM invariant to ensure deterministic price discovery from block zero without requiring creator seed capital:

// Invariant Equation:
(RETH + VETH) × RToken = K

Where:
• VETH = 0.50 ETH (Virtual liquidity anchor)
• RToken,0 = 800,000,000 tokens (Tokens deposited in curve)
• K = 0.50 × 800,000,000 × 1036 = 4.0 × 1044

Spot Price Equation

The marginal price of the token in ETH at any point along the curve is given by:

Price(RETH) = (VETH + RETH)2 / K
  • Genesis Price: ~0.000000000625 ETH (~$0.0000026 USD | ~$2,625 initial Market Cap)
  • Graduation Price: ~0.000000015625 ETH (~$0.0000656 USD | ~$65,625 graduation Market Cap)

04. Native ETH Reflection Engine

Robin Pump eliminates the security flaws of legacy rebase tokens by using a MasterChef-style Cumulative Accumulator in pure native ETH.

accEthPerSharet = accEthPerSharet-1 + (HolderTaxFee × 1018) / CirculatingTokens

UserPendingReward = (UserTokenBalance × accEthPerShare) / 1018 - UserRewardDebt

Dividends accumulate on every curve swap where a Holder Tax is enabled (0% to 5%). Holders can call claimHolderRewards() anytime via the Portfolio tab and receive ETH directly to their wallet.

05. Uniswap V4 Migration & Hook Integration

When the real ETH reserve reaches the immutable 2.0 ETH milestone:

  1. Creator Bounty: 0.02 ETH is transferred automatically to the token creator wallet as an incentive.
  2. Liquidity Migration: The remaining 1.98 ETH and 200,000,000 reserved tokens are transferred to the Uniswap V4 Migration Router.
  3. Uniswap V4 Singleton Hook: The pool is initialized on the Uniswap V4 Pool Manager with the protocol's custom hook (RobinhoodBondingCurveHook).
  4. Permanent Lock: LP positions are permanently locked into the contract or burned, mathematically guaranteeing that liquidity can never be rug-pulled.
